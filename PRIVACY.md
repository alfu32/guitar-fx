# Privacy policy

guitar-fx processes audio on your computer and does not collect, store, send or share personal data.

- **Audio:** the sound from your selected input device is processed in memory and played to your selected
  output device. It is not recorded to disk, except what you record yourself with the looper, which stays in
  memory until you clear it or close the app. It is never transmitted.
- **Settings:** your last effect chain, chosen devices, saved chains and impulse responses are plain files in
  your user configuration folder (`~/.config/guitar-fx/`, or the Windows and macOS equivalent). You can
  delete them at any time.
- **Network:** the app makes no network connections, has no accounts, analytics or advertising, and
  includes no third-party tracking.
- **Microphone permission:** the operating system may ask for microphone access because guitar interfaces are
  audio input devices. It is used only to hear your instrument.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/alfu32/guitar-fx/issues.
